§175

risograph printing

20.5 × 27.3 cm

2024

While exploring queer representation in cinema, I discovered Different from the Others—one of the first movies to portray homosexuality with empathy rather than ridicule. Most copies were tragically destroyed by the Nazis, leaving behind only fragments. This loss inspired me to spotlight the movie's significance through research and storytelling. By weaving its history with global parallels, the project aims to make its message accessible and relevant to contemporary audiences.

QUEIR

type design

13 × 23 cm

2023

Through unconventional letterforms and unexpected shapes, this typeface celebrates the beauty of being queer and existing outside of the expected. The flowing, organic forms of Queir represent the flamboyance and fluidity of queer people, while the smaller spikes reflect the protective walls we build to navigate and survive a society that does not always make space for us.

Norm

typographic poster

84.1 × 118.9 cm

2023

This poster sheds light on the overshadowing and repression of queer voices by patriarchal*, misogynist*, colonial* structures. A few students created the awareness campaign featuring NORM collaboratively, we exchanged and reworked each other’s ideas in multiple rounds.

Reshaping Futures

mixed-media installation

460 × 340 × 160 cm

2024

This work captures the tension between destruction and regeneration. A large-scale mural depicts the ruins of a failing system gradually transforming into roots and a thriving ecosystem. Overlaid on this surface, a multimodal video collage intensifies the contrast.

Walpurgisnacht

vector graphics

1359 × 1922 px, 1080 × 1080 px

2023

An animated moving poster and series of Instagram teasers for Walpurgisnacht, taking the viewer from a dark, eerie atmosphere into a bright and playful one. Animated in After Effects, the visuals shift through contrasting colour themes to reflect the transformation from night into day.

How To Zine

2026

This graduation project is a space that explores zines as tools for sharing knowledge, building community and learning through making.
